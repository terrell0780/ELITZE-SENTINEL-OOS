## 🎯 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Persona Breakdown

1. **MSP & MSSP Owners / CEOs**:
   - *Core Pain Point*: Commoditization of standard managed IT services; client demand for AI capabilities without the budget or talent to build internal LLM infrastructure.
   - *Winning Hook*: Instant productization of a 30-hub AI console under their own brand for an asset purchase of $10k–$35k vs. $100k+ in custom software engineering.
2. **VP of Technology / Practice Leads**:
   - *Core Pain Point*: Public sector and healthcare clients in BC demanding strict compliance with British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act (FIPPA).
   - *Winning Hook*: 100% sovereign data architecture supporting local on-prem Ollama/vLLM inference where zero customer data leaves Canadian borders.
3. **Cybersecurity Directors & SOC Leads**:
   - *Core Pain Point*: Manual translation of emerging CVEs and MITRE ATT&CK techniques into actionable SIEM detection rules.
   - *Winning Hook*: Pre-engineered cybersecurity threat intelligence hub that automatically compiles attack matrices into Splunk SPL and Microsoft Sentinel KQL queries.
