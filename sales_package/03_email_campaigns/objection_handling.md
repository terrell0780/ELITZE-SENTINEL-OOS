**Buyer Intent:** International acquirers (US, EU, Asia) may worry that the Canadian Internet Registration Authority (CIRA) `.ca` residency rules will block domain transfer.

**Scripted Response:**
> "International buyers routinely acquire and manage high-value `.ca` domains through four straightforward, fully compliant mechanisms recognized by CIRA:
> 1. **Registrar Trustee / Nominee Service**: Major accredited registrars (including Dan.com, Sedo, and Webnames.ca) provide automated, seamless Canadian Presence Trustee services during checkout at nominal or zero additional cost.
> 2. **Canadian Trademark / Pending Application**: If your entity owns a registered trademark in Canada matching the domain name, you satisfy CIRA Canadian Presence requirements immediately.
> 3. **Canadian Corporate Entity or Subsidiary**: Any registered federal or provincial corporation, partnership, or holding subsidiary satisfies CIRA requirements.
> 4. **Escrow.com Facilitated Transfer**: We manage the CIRA Registrant Contact update directly through Escrow.com, ensuring a smooth, fully compliant transfer to your chosen registrar account.
> 
> We have completed this process numerous times and will handle the technical registrar coordination during escrow onboarding."
