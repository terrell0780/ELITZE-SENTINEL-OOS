## 🌐 Global Campaign Operational & Compliance Rules

1. **CAN-SPAM Act (United States)**: Every outbound message must include a truthful subject line, clear sender identification, valid physical postal address, and a functional 1-click opt-out or reply opt-out mechanism honored within 10 business days.
2. **GDPR / ePrivacy (European Union / UK)**: Outreach to EU/UK corporate contacts must rely on legitimate interest in B2B corporate acquisition opportunities, with immediate opt-out compliance and zero retention of personal data following an unsubscribe request.
3. **CASL Compliance (Canada)**: When targeting Canadian prospects via global lists, ensure compliance under CASL Section 6(6) conspicuous publication standards.
4. **CRM Suppression Integration**: Any prospect replying "UNSUBSCRIBE", "STOP", or "OPT-OUT" must be immediately added to the master suppression list across all campaign platforms (Lemlist, Instantly, Apollo).
