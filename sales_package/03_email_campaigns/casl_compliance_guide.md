# Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) Compliance Guide

**Applies to:** B2B Email Outreach targeting businesses in Victoria, BC, Vancouver, BC, and across Canada for the acquisition of `elitze.ca` and Elitze Sentinel software assets.

---

## ⚖️ Executive Summary & Statutory Scope

Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL, S.C. 2010, c. 23) is one of the world's most rigorous electronic communication laws. It applies to any Commercial Electronic Message (CEM) sent to an electronic address (email, instant messaging, SMS, social media direct messaging) originating from or delivered to a Canadian computer system.

Violations of CASL are strictly enforced by the **Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission (CRTC)**, with substantial Administrative Monetary Penalties (AMPs):
- **Corporate Entities**: Up to **$10,000,000 CAD** per statutory violation.
- **Individual Senders**: Up to **$1,000,000 CAD** per violation.
- **Officer & Director Liability**: Corporate officers, directors, and agents who direct, authorize, assent to, or participate in the commission of a violation are personally liable.

To maintain 100% legal compliance and institutional integrity during the `elitze.ca` sales campaign, all outbound outreach must strictly adhere to the statutory frameworks outlined below.

## 📊 CASL Consent Classification Matrix

CASL strictly prohibits sending CEMs without valid **Express Consent** or **Implied Consent / Statutory Exemption**. The matrix below defines the legal categories governing our B2B sales outreach:

| Consent Category | Statutory Basis | Legal Definition & Criteria | Validity Duration | Application to `elitze.ca` Campaign |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Express Consent** | CASL § 10(1) | Recipient explicitly agreed to receive CEMs via an affirmative opt-in action (e.g., website contact form, demo request checkbox, signed NDA). | Valid indefinitely until the recipient explicitly revokes consent / unsubscribes. | Inbound leads from `elitze.ca` contact form, Acquire.com buyer inquiries, and direct demo bookings. |
| **Implied Consent: Existing Business Relationship (EBR)** | CASL § 10(9)(a) | Recipient has purchased an asset, entered a contract, or engaged in business with the sender. | Valid for **2 years (24 months)** from the date of the transaction or contract. | Existing clients, previous software buyers, or historical consulting partners. |
| **Implied Consent: Inquiry / Application** | CASL § 10(9)(b) | Recipient made an inquiry or application regarding an asset or service within the preceding 6 months. | Valid for **6 months (180 days)** from the date of the inquiry. | Prospective buyers who requested the technical dossier or pricing details via email. |
| **B2B Conspicuously Published Exemption** | **CASL § 6(6) / § 10(9)(b)** | **Primary Outbound Legal Basis**: Recipient has conspicuously published their business email address (e.g. on corporate website, directory, LinkedIn page) **WITHOUT** an accompanying statement that they do not wish to receive unsolicited CEMs, **AND** the message directly pertains to their business role or organizational functions. | Valid as long as the email remains conspicuously published without a non-solicitation disclaimer. | **All cold outbound B2B emails** to BC MSP owners, CTOs, CSOs, and tech directors regarding AI OS acquisition. |
| **B2B Inter-Organizational Exemption** | CASL GIC Regs § 3(a)(ii) | CEM sent between employees or representatives of organizations that have an ongoing relationship, where message is relevant to the recipient organization. | Valid duration of organizational relationship. | Outreach between strategic corporate partners and established tech alliances. |

## 📋 Statutory Mandatory Email Disclosure Requirements

Every outbound B2B commercial electronic message sent during this campaign **MUST** contain four statutory disclosure elements directly in the body/footer of the message:

1. **Clear Sender Identification**:
   - The sender's full legal name or operating trade name (`[Your Name]`, `Owner / Lead Architect`).
   - The business name and legal entity (`Elitze Sentinel`, `elitze.ca`).
2. **Valid Physical Postal Address**:
   - A complete physical street address or registered postal box in Canada or current operating jurisdiction (`[Your Physical Business Address, Victoria / Vancouver, BC, Postal Code, Canada]`). Must be valid for at least 60 days post-send.
3. **Direct Contact Information**:
   - A direct, working electronic contact method (e.g., direct email `acquire@elitze.ca`, phone number, and website URL `https://elitze.ca`).
4. **Clear & Functional Unsubscribe Mechanism**:
   - An easy-to-use, functional mechanism allowing the recipient to opt out at zero cost (e.g., *"Reply 'UNSUBSCRIBE' at any time to be immediately removed"* or a 1-click unsubscribe hyperlink).
   - The unsubscribe link/mechanism must remain operational for at least **60 days** after the message is sent.

## ⏱️ 10-Day Statutory Opt-Out Processing & Recordkeeping SLA

CASL § 11(3) mandates that any unsubscribe request or opt-out notice must be processed and fully operationalized **without delay, and in no event later than 10 business days** after receiving the request.

### Operational Policy for `elitze.ca` Outreach:
- **Our Internal SLA: 24-Hour Processing**: All opt-out requests (replies containing "STOP", "UNSUBSCRIBE", "OPT-OUT", or "REMOVE") must be processed within **24 hours** of receipt.
- **Global Suppression Synchronization**: Once an opt-out is received, the email domain and specific recipient address are immediately added to the master suppression list across all sales tools (Apollo, Lemlist, Instantly, CRM).
- **Zero Re-Contact Rule**: Never send a follow-up or confirmation email once an unsubscribe is processed (sending a "You have been unsubscribed" email is itself considered a violation under CRTC interpretations).

## 🛡️ Operational Pre-Send Compliance Checklist

Before executing any outbound email batch targeting Canadian tech executives, the sales operator must complete the following 6-point verification gate:

- [ ] **1. Conspicuous Publication Verification**: Has the recipient's business email address been verified on their public corporate website, team directory, or official professional profile?
- [ ] **2. Negative Statement Check**: Has the recipient's website/profile been inspected to ensure there is **NO** "No unsolicited commercial messages / No sales solicitations" disclaimer?
- [ ] **3. Business Role Relevance**: Is the acquisition of `elitze.ca` (an enterprise AI & cybersecurity OS) directly relevant to the recipient's official professional capacity (e.g., CTO, VP Technology, Head of Cyber, MSP Owner)?
- [ ] **4. Mandatory Footer Inclusion**: Does the email footer include Sender Name, Business Name, Physical Postal Address, Contact Email/Phone, and the CASL Section 6(6) statutory disclosure?
- [ ] **5. Unsubscribe Functionality**: Is the unsubscribe mechanism (Reply "UNSUBSCRIBE" or 1-click link) fully functional and monitored daily?
- [ ] **6. Master Suppression Scrub**: Has the prospect list been scrubbed against our master suppression list to ensure no prior opt-outs are contacted?

## 🔒 Interplay with Canadian Privacy Laws (PIPEDA & BC PIPA)

In addition to CASL compliance, all prospect data handling must respect Canadian privacy legislation:
1. **BC Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)**:
   - Governs the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data by private sector organizations in British Columbia.
   - *Business Contact Information Exemption*: PIPA explicitly permits the collection and use of an individual's business contact information (name, position, business telephone, business address, business email) solely for the purpose of communicating with them in relation to their employment, business, or profession.
2. **Federal PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act)**:
   - Aligns with CASL requirements; mandates that business contact data must be secured, not sold to unauthorized third parties, and deleted upon legitimate request.
3. **Data Retention & Audit Trail**:
   - Maintain timestamped records of where each lead's email was discovered (URL source) for a minimum of **3 years** to satisfy potential CRTC compliance audit requirements.
